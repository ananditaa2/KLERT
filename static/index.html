Key Project Features

Designed to be simple, clear, and easy to present.

Visual Heatmap Highlights

Highlights the specific area on the scan image that the model examined to make its decision.

Multiple Computer Models

Compares 3 standard computer vision neural networks (Custom CNN, ResNet-18, VGG-16).

Interactive Opacity Slider

Easily slide the opacity to see the highlighted heatmap right on top of the original MRI image.

Simple Summary Reports
